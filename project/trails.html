How we rate difficulty

Difficulty levels and what they mean
LevelWhat to expectGood for
EasyMostly flat or gently rolling ground on clear paths, with little exposure to sun or wind.Beginners, families and a first outing in new boots
ModerateSteady climbs on uneven ground. Expect sun, wind or loose rock, and a long morning or most of a day.Hikers with some experience and good fitness
ChallengingSteep, long or high-altitude routes, often with a guide, early starts and several days of planning.Experienced hikers who are fit and well prepared

Distances, climbs and times are approximate and change with the route you take and the season. Confirm access, fees and conditions with the park, forest authority or a local guide before you go.
