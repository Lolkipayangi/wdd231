Site Name

Rift Trails Kenya

  • The name tells visitors the site is about hiking trails. "Rift" points to the Great Rift Valley, which shapes many of Kenya's best-known hikes, and "Kenya" makes the location clear.
  • It is short, easy to remember, and easy to fit into a logo.

Optional domain availability: rifttrailskenya.org (availability not yet checked)

Site Purpose

The site is a hub for hikers in Kenya. It will provide:

  • A searchable list of trails, with the location, difficulty, length, and elevation gain of each one.
  • Details for each trail in a pop-up window, including the best season to go and what to bring.
  • A membership form for joining group hikes and receiving trail updates.

The site will have three pages: Home, Trails, and Join. A separate form action page will display the submitted form data.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience (beginner to intermediate hikers, locals and tourists) may ask:

  1. Which trails near Nairobi are good for a beginner on a half-day hike?
  2. How hard is a particular trail, and how long will it take?
  3. When is the best time of year to hike a particular trail?
  4. How do I join a group hike or get in touch with the club?

Questions 1 to 3 are answered by the Trails page and its data. Question 4 is answered by the Join page.
