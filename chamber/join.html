NP Membership benefits

  • Free listing in the online business directory
  • Invitations to community and networking events
  • Access to chamber training workshops
  • Use of the chamber meeting hall for outreach events

Bronze Membership benefits

  • Standard listing in the online business directory
  • Invitations to community and networking events
  • 10% discount on chamber-hosted training sessions
  • Monthly chamber newsletter feature eligibility

Silver Membership benefits

  • Enhanced directory listing with photos and links
  • 20% discount on chamber events and training
  • Quarterly spotlight placement on the chamber home page
  • Priority booking for the chamber meeting hall

Gold Membership benefits

  • Premium directory listing at the top of search results
  • Free entry to all chamber events and training sessions
  • Monthly spotlight placement on the chamber home page
  • Dedicated chamber advocacy for permits and licensing support
